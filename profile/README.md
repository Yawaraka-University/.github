## About

Yawaraka University is built on a single conviction: *suppleness is strength.* Questioning fixed assumptions, framing a problem from more than one perspective, and adapting one's thinking to circumstances are, in our view, what make knowledge hold up in a rapidly changing world.

Founded in 1947 with two faculties, Science and Agriculture, the University has grown into a comprehensive institution of fifteen faculties, two graduate schools, and four research institutes.

This organization is where that conviction is put into practice. It hosts the research software, experimental records, and publications produced at the University, so that every reported result can be traced back to the code and records behind it.

## Graduate schools

**Graduate School of Science, Engineering and Informatics** — integrates the Faculties of Science, Engineering, and Information Science to train the next generation of researchers and engineers where mathematics, materials, energy, and information technology meet.

- Master's program (2 years): Mathematical Physics · Mechanical and Electrical Systems · Information and Intelligence
- Doctoral program (3 years): Advanced Science and Technology · Computational Science
- Research fields: quantum computing, deep learning, robotics, materials informatics

**Graduate School of Life, Agricultural and Medical Sciences** — integrates the Faculties of Agriculture and Medicine to study the mechanisms of food, life, and disease from multiple angles.

- Master's program (2 years): Agricultural and Life Sciences · Applied Biochemistry · Medical Science
- Doctoral program (3 years): Integrated Life and Agricultural Sciences · Medical Research
- Research fields: bioinformatics, genomic medicine, food function science, environmental toxicology

## This organization

| **Publications** | Technical reports and papers, kept with their LaTeX sources and the scripts that generate their figures. |

### Research principles

**Reproducibility.** Each repository documents its environment and the commands that run its experiments and tests. Where a full reproduction is not possible from a fresh clone, the repository states so and explains why.

**Honest reporting.** Results are reported together with their limitations. Exploratory comparisons on development data are kept distinct from independent evaluation, and negative results are recorded rather than discarded.

**Sound evaluation.** Selection rules are fixed in advance wherever possible, and evaluation splits are designed to prevent leakage between reference and test data.

**Respect for rights and privacy.** Copyrighted media, private reference data, and personal information are never redistributed. Repositories publish code, aggregated results, and documentation instead.

### Citing our work

If you build on software or results from this organization, please cite the corresponding paper or technical report. Where no paper is available, cite the repository together with the commit you used.
